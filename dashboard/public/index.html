Campaigns: different emails, same infrastructure estimate

What am I seeing: domains from risky emails, linked when they share rare infrastructure (name server, IP, certificate, registration day).

Why it matters: emails that look unrelated can come from one attacker. Blocking the shared infrastructure stops the whole campaign.

How to read this: each dot is a domain, colored by the worst verdict of its emails. A thicker line means more shared rare infrastructure. Huge shared services like Cloudflare count almost nothing.
